about yuko

Yuko cooks the way she reads — slowly, attentively, with the instinct that everything connects if you hold it long enough.

Her dinners happen in a room surrounded by thirty years of books, records, and accumulated evidence of a life spent paying attention. The food is Japanese in its bones and New York in its accidents.

No fixed menu. No repeats. She follows what arrived at the market that morning, what felt right in the afternoon.
